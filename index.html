About the book, in brief

Hayat al-Hayawan al-Kubra (“The Great Life of Animals”) is a 700-year-old Arabic encyclopedia by Kamal al-Din al-Damiri (1341–1405). Its entries mix nature, poetry, proverbs, history and belief — and this site retells them for a new generation, treating medieval claims as historical lore, not modern zoological fact.
