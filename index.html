📚 About the Book

A 700-year-old menagerie.

Hayat al-Hayawan al-Kubra (“The Great Life of Animals”) is an Arabic animal encyclopedia organized alphabetically by animal name. Its entries mix observations of nature with poetry, proverbs, religious traditions, medicine, dreams, history, and the meanings hidden inside words.

Its author, Kamal al-Din Muhammad al-Damiri (1341–1405), was an Egyptian scholar associated with Cairo. Trained in Islamic law and Arabic learning, he gathered material from hundreds of earlier works and shaped it into a vast reference book whose animals often open doors into human culture.

This website retells those old accounts for a modern audience while clearly treating medieval claims as historical lore—not established zoological fact.
